Cleaner cities, measurable impact

EcoPulse turns everyday waste habits into data that cities can act on.

Mission

Make waste segregation easy, rewarding and verifiable for every household and every ward.

Vision

Zero-landfill Indian cities where citizens, workers and administrators share one source of truth.

Impact

Live figures for collections, resolved complaints and EcoCredits issued appear in the Analytics view.

Communities

Any city ward, school, college or hostel can start its own community, so local heads resolve local problems directly.
